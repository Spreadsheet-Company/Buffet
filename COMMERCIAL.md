## What a commercial license is for

A commercial license from Spreadsheet Company releases the licensee from
the AGPL's publication condition for the code the company holds the
copyright in, for a use the agreement names. The uses it is meant for:

- a private fork or a branded edition whose changes are not published;
- the game embedded in, or hosted as part of, a product or a service whose
  own source is not offered to its users;
- the game bundled with proprietary software under terms the AGPL does not
  permit;
- any use for which a compliance department will not accept the AGPL.

A commercial license covers the game's code. It does not cover the content
licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0 by contributors, which stays under those terms for
everyone, licensee included; it does not cover the engine or the language,
which are Apache-2.0 and need no license of this kind; and it grants no
right in the names, which `TRADEMARK.md` governs.
