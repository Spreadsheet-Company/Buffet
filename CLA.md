## Why there is one

The game's code is licensed to everyone under the AGPL-3.0-or-later, and
Spreadsheet Company also offers it under a commercial license to those who
want out of the AGPL's terms (`COMMERCIAL.md`). The company can offer only
what it holds the rights to. This agreement is what lets a contribution be
offered both ways, and it promises in return that the contribution stays
available to everyone under the public terms.

## How to sign

Once, in the first pull request that touches the game's code, add a comment
or a commit trailer reading:

```text
I have read CLA.md at <commit> and agree to it: <your name> <your email>
```

naming the commit of this file you read. Every commit still carries the
`Signed-off-by:` line of the Developer Certificate of Origin. The project
keeps the record in the pull request.
